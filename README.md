# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing coffee shop sales data from January to June 2023 across three store locations: Astoria, Hell's Kitchen, and Lower Manhattan.

The objective was to explore sales performance, identify revenue trends, evaluate product demand, and understand customer purchasing patterns.

Using Microsoft Excel, I cleaned and analysed the dataset and developed an interactive dashboard to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning and Preparation
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Excel Formulas
- Interactive Slicers
- Data Visualisation
- Business Insights and Recommendations

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Revenue Growth
Revenue increased significantly between February and June 2023, with June recording the highest monthly revenue of approximately $166,486.

### 2. Store Performance
Hell's Kitchen generated the highest revenue at $236,511.17, followed by Astoria and Lower Manhattan.

However, revenue contributions across the three locations were relatively balanced.

### 3. Product Performance
Coffee and tea were the leading revenue-generating categories, contributing approximately 66.7% of total revenue.

### 4. Peak Sales Hours
Sales activity was strongest during the morning hours, particularly around 9 AM to 10 AM.

### 5. Weekly Sales Pattern
Monday recorded the highest total revenue, while Sunday generated comparatively lower sales.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Investigate the factors contributing to monthly revenue growth to support better sales forecasting.
- Compare customer demand and purchasing patterns across store locations.
- Maintain adequate inventory of high-demand coffee and tea products.
- Explore complementary product bundles to encourage additional purchases.
- Optimise staffing and inventory preparation during peak sales hours.
- Analyse average daily sales before introducing targeted promotions for quieter periods.

## 🎯 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Microsoft Excel can be used to clean, analyse, and visualise sales data to support informed business decisions.

The interactive dashboard provides insights into revenue performance, customer purchasing behaviour, and product demand, highlighting opportunities for operational improvement and business growth.
